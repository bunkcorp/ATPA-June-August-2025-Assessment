# ATPA Exam References - Comprehensive Guide

## Overview
This document organizes key references for the ATPA (Actuarial Technical Professional Assessment) exam, categorized by priority and relevance to different assessment tasks.

---

## 🚨 HIGHEST PRIORITY (Must Read)

### **Actuarial Standards Board (ASB) Documents**

#### **ASOP No. 41 - Actuarial Communications**
- **Source**: [Actuarial Standards Board](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/actuarial-communications/)
- **Purpose**: Establishes standards for effective actuarial communication
- **Key Requirements**:
  - Clear, accurate, and complete communication
  - Appropriate disclosure of assumptions and limitations
  - Documentation of methods and procedures
  - Professional presentation standards
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for Task 6 (Executive Summary)

#### **ASOP on Data Quality**
- **Source**: [Actuarial Standards Board](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/data-quality/)
- **Focus**: Standards for data quality assessment and documentation
- **Key Points**:
  - Data validation procedures
  - Documentation of data sources and limitations
  - Quality control measures
  - Data governance requirements
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for Task 1 (Data Preparation) and Task 2 (Privacy Analysis)

#### **ASOP on Modeling**
- **Source**: [Actuarial Standards Board](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/modeling-3/)
- **Scope**: Standards for actuarial modeling practices
- **Key Requirements**:
  - Model validation and testing
  - Documentation of model assumptions
  - Sensitivity analysis
  - Model governance and controls
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for Tasks 3-5 (Modeling tasks)

### **Fairness and Bias in AI/ML**

#### **Frees & Huang (2021) - "The Discriminating (Pricing) Actuary"**
- **Source**: [North American Actuarial Journal](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10920277.2021.1951296)
- **Key Findings**:
  - Analysis of fairness in insurance pricing
  - Methods for detecting and mitigating bias
  - Regulatory considerations for fair pricing
  - Statistical approaches to fairness assessment
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for fairness considerations in Module 3

#### **NAIC AI Principles**
- **Source**: [National Association of Insurance Commissioners](https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/call_materials/Attachment_A_AI_Principles.pdf)
- **Key Principles**:
  - Fair and ethical AI use
  - Accountability and governance
  - Transparency and explainability
  - Regulatory compliance
  - Consumer protection
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for AI/ML applications in insurance

### **Privacy and Data Protection Laws**

#### **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**
- **Source**: [GDPR.eu](https://gdpr.eu/what-is-gdpr/)
- **Key Principles**:
  - Lawful, fair, and transparent processing
  - Purpose limitation and data minimization
  - Right to be forgotten
  - Data portability
  - Consent requirements
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for Task 2 (Privacy Analysis)

#### **HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)**
- **Source**: [HHS.gov](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html)
- **Scope**: Health information privacy and security
- **Key Requirements**:
  - Protected health information (PHI) safeguards
  - Privacy rule compliance
  - Security rule requirements
  - Breach notification requirements
- **Assessment Relevance**: **CRITICAL** for healthcare-related data
