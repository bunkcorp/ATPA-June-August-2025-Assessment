
TASK 2: PRIVACY & ETHICS ANALYSIS

2a) BENEFITS AND RISKS OF DEMOGRAPHIC DATA:

BENEFITS:
- Identifies systemic disparities in arrest patterns
- Enables evidence-based policy discussions
- Supports bias detection and mitigation
- Helps with resource allocation and planning

RISKS:
- May perpetuate existing biases in law enforcement
- Privacy violations and civil rights concerns
- Potential for misinterpretation and misuse
- Data quality and representation issues

2b) PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS COMPLIANCE:

ATPA MODULE 1 ETHICS FRAMEWORK:
- FAIRNESS: Implement bias testing across demographic groups
- SAFETY: Protect vulnerable populations from harm
- TRANSPARENCY: Document all limitations and assumptions
- ACCOUNTABILITY: Establish oversight and governance

PREVENTION MEASURES:
- Independent review board with community representation
- Regular bias audits and fairness monitoring
- Clear documentation and communication standards
- Legal compliance with anti-discrimination laws
- Ongoing community engagement and feedback
